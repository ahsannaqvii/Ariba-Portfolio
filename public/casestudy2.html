CASE STUDY

Project Overview

This project involved designing and developing a family-focused Dhikr (Zikr) tracking application using Glide, a no-code platform. The app was created for a client who wanted a simple way to encourage her children to build a consistent habit of daily remembrance through personalized Dhikr assignments, motivational messages, and progress tracking.

GOALS

Objectives

The goal was to design an intuitive Dhikr app that encourages children to build consistent daily habits while giving parents full administrative control. The experience focused on child-friendly interactions, secure content management, customizable Dhikr libraries, and positive reinforcement through daily streaks and motivational messages.

Creative Process

The design process began by understanding the client's vision of encouraging children to develop a daily spiritual routine. User flows were mapped separately for parents and children to ensure each experience remained simple and purposeful.

RESULTS

Conclusion

The final application delivers a meaningful digital experience that supports daily Islamic practice in an engaging and accessible way. By balancing a child-friendly interface with a powerful parent management system, the app makes it easy for families to build consistent Dhikr habits while allowing content to grow and adapt over time through a fully manageable no-code solution.
