CASE STUDY

Project Overview

The Dbiz Solutions website was designed and developed to establish a premium digital identity for VConn's subsidiary brand. I led the project from wireframing and UI design to front-end development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, creating a modern, high-end web experience that showcases the company's services, products, and expertise through a refined black-and-white visual aesthetic.
