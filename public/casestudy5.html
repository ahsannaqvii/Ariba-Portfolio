CASE STUDY

Project Overview

This project involved designing a modern e-commerce platform for a technology retailer in Figma. The focus was on creating a seamless shopping experience supported by well-defined user flows, intuitive navigation, and a scalable design system. Alongside high-fidelity interfaces, I developed reusable components and a comprehensive style guide to ensure consistency, efficiency, and future scalability across the platform.
